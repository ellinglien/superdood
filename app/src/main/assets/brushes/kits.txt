# Tool kits: the brushes one way of working needs, put on the bar together from the Tools page (its Kits chip,
# or Load Kit under This Brush when the brush shown is in a kit). A kit fills the bar's first slots, in this order.
#
# A kit starts with "Kit: Its Name", then "Intro: one whole sentence" saying how the kit is used, in order.
# Then one line a brush (a kit loads in the tray's order, kind by kind, A to Z, whatever order the lines are in; only Default Tools keeps its own): the Basic tool's name exactly as on the Tools page, More Brushes too (or one of the
# bar's first-launch brushes, ToolSlots.FIRST, by its name), a colon,
# and its job in two or three words ("Sumi: Drop ink"). The bar's slot says "1 Drop ink" when it is picked.
# A last line "Eraser Layer 1: Erase layer 1" also sets the eraser to that layer as the kit loads
# (Everything, Pen, Paint or Dry Media). At most nine brushes a kit (the bar's slots; Fill in has its own place beside the eraser). Lines starting with # are notes.
# Plain, warm words, no semicolons (docs/TEXT-STYLE.md).
