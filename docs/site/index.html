About Me

Hi! I'm Elling Lien, an artist and arts administrator from Newfoundland and Labrador.

I'm Executive Director of the Nickel Independent Film Festival in St. John's, and of Unpossible NL, which runs the RPM Challenge to make a record in February. Thousands of records have been made as part of it since 2006. I'm also proud to be on the board of directors of Lawnya Vawnya, a really neat arts and culture festival in St. John's.

I'm on Instagram as @ellinglien.

Other things I've made and shared on GitHub:

  • NYT Games for Supernote: the crossword and Wordle, tuned for e-ink.
  • sssketch: a stem arranger for Endlesss jams.
  • Thermy: printing to little Bluetooth thermal printers.
