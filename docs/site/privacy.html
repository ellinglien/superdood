Superdood

Privacy

Superdood is made by one person: me, Elling Lien, in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. I'm responsible for the data on this page. Write to me at ellinglien@gmail.com.

What leaves your tablet

Superdood works without the internet. It sends something only when you use one of these:

  • The Prompts notebook and the Fridge. Once you turn it on, it asks superdood.org for each week's #inktober52 word. When you choose Send to the Fridge, it sends the picture of that page, the name and caption you typed, and your tablet's key: a random code made for the Fridge, not your tablet's serial number.
  • Your email, the first time you send to the Fridge, so I can send you a link that confirms it.
  • Draw Together. The strokes you draw pass through superdood.org to the people you share the page with. Nothing is kept after the session.
  • Brush Studio's library. A brush you choose to share, with the name and note you give it.
  • Read Handwriting. It uses Google's ML Kit, which downloads Google's handwriting model once and sends Google usage statistics. Your handwriting stays on the tablet.

Send on Wi-Fi, to a phone or computer, stays on your own Wi-Fi.

What I keep, and for how long

  • Your email and tablet key: until you choose Stop Taking Part, or a year after your tablet last sent a page. Then they're deleted, with your pages on the Fridge.
  • Your pages on the Fridge: until you take one down, choose Stop Taking Part, or I remove it.
  • No analytics, no ads, no cookies. The server doesn't keep your address. It holds a scrambled form of it in memory only, to limit how often anyone can send, and forgets it when it restarts. If something goes wrong, the web server's error log may note an address for a short while.

Your pages on the tablet are yours. Nothing here ever deletes them.

Why

  • Taking part in the Fridge: your consent, given when you turn the Prompts notebook on and confirm your email. You can take it back any time with Stop Taking Part.
  • Blocking someone who breaks the terms: my legitimate interest in keeping the Fridge safe for everyone.

Who else handles it

Amazon Web Services runs the server, in the United States, and sends the confirmation email, from Canada, under AWS's data processing terms. Nobody else gets it. I never sell or share your data, and I use your email only to confirm it and, if I have to, to block it.

If you're in the EU or UK: the transfer to the United States is covered by AWS's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Canada has an EU adequacy decision.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I keep, to object to it, or to have a copy. Stop Taking Part deletes it at once. Otherwise write to me, and I'll answer within a month. In the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Age

You must be 16 or older to send to the Fridge.
